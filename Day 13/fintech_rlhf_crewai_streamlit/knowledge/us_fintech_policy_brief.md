# U.S. Fintech Policy Brief

This demo uses a U.S. compliance lens for fintech AI workflows.

## Reference anchors

- CFPB adverse-action notice requirements apply to credit decisions and require accurate, specific principal reasons for adverse action.
- Federal Reserve, OCC, and FDIC model risk management guidance emphasizes governance, validation, documentation, and risk-based controls.
- FINRA reminds members that generative AI and LLM tools do not remove regulatory obligations.

## Demo design implications

- Keep explanations plain-English and reviewable.
- Make every decision auditable.
- Store human feedback with timestamps and reviewer notes.
- Flag model uncertainty and route borderline cases to a human reviewer.
